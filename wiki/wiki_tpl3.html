__RIDES__

Battling NPC trainers

You are in exactly one series at a time, and it is per player. Trainers only appear for players whose active series they belong to, so two people standing in the same place can see different trainers. The three series are Kanto (radicalred), Sinnoh (bdsp) and Unbound. New players start in no series at all until they pick one from a League NPC, which is why a fresh player sees none of this.

It also means gym structures are series-specific: a Kanto gym generates for everyone, but only a Kanto-series player will find a leader inside it.

__MULCH__

Spawn mechanics

Most useful fact: Pokémon never spawn within 16 blocks of you - they appear 16-40 blocks away, and the biome is checked there, not where you stand. Standing in a small biome means most spawns roll on the neighbouring one.

Mega, Dynamax, Tera and Z-moves

None of this is explained in game, and all of it is craftable. Mega Evolution needs two things at once — the Pokémon must hold its own Mega Stone and you must be wearing a Mega Bracelet. Miss either and the mod says so: “No mega bracelet” or “Don’t have the correct stone”. 81 Pokémon have a stone, and the Pokédex entry names which one.

__MEGAMINING__

Light: can I put torches down?

Almost always yes. Two different numbers get called “light”, and only one of them is affected by torches:
  • Sky light — how much daylight reaches the spot. 2,023 spawn entries use this. Torches, lanterns and glowstone do not change it at all. Light up a cave as much as you like; the spawns are unaffected.
  • Light level — the total, torches included. Only 14 entries in the whole pack use it.
So if an entry says sky light, torch freely. The only ones that genuinely care: Gastly, Haunter and Gengar (need light level 0 — a single torch stops them), Misdreavus, Mismagius, Morelull and Shiinotic (7 or under), Zekrom (7 or under) and Reshiram, which is the odd one out and wants it bright (8–15).

This is why a dripstone cave hunt is safe to light up — nothing in the dripstone pool checks torch light.

“Needs X nearby” — the radius is tiny

Some Pokémon only spawn near a particular block. The check is 4 blocks horizontally and 2 up or down — and it is measured from the spawn position, not from you. Since spawns roll 16–40 blocks away, a single block out in the world is almost never close enough to whichever spot the game picks. Build a dense patch rather than placing one.

Worked example — Pumpkaboo. Its biome line says any overworld biome, which is true and useless. What actually gates it: night, a pumpkin or carved pumpkin within those 4 blocks, and a natural ground block. Carving is not required, and farmland is explicitly excluded — a tilled pumpkin farm blocks the very spawn you are farming for. Lay pumpkins on grass or dirt in a block, stand 16–40 blocks off, and wait for night.

Known issues and traps

__NOMODEL__
Converting a charged urn to its Galarian form resets the progress. The Galarian urns do work - they charge and summon normally - but they need 75 defeats, not 50, and they change the required type: Storms Electric→Fighting, Embers Fire→Dark, Frost Ice→Psychic. Craft the Galarian version before you start charging, not after.
37 Legendary Monuments quests are unfinished. Their quest text is literally "Work in Progress...", so those LM routes do nothing — a mod-side stub, not a server misconfiguration. Mewtwo is the one people hit first (use Myths & Legends' dr_fujis_diary instead), but the list also includes Kyogre, Groudon, Rayquaza, Jirachi, Deoxys, Shaymin, the weather trio, Genesect, Zygarde, Diancie, Volcanion, Type: Null and Silvally, all four Tapus, Necrozma, Magearna, Zeraora, Kubfu and Urshifu, Zarude, Enamorus, Koraidon and Miraidon, the Loyal Three, Ogerpon, Terapagos and Pecharunt. Most are still obtainable another way — check the Pokédex entry before spending a one-shot key item on an LM pedestal.
Wardens never dig back down while you are watching them — and that is not a bug. A Warden leaves by burrowing, which needs 60 uninterrupted seconds with nothing it can detect. Any living thing it senses resets that timer to a full 60. Cobblemon spawns Pokémon in a ring 16–40 blocks around you, up to 8 a second — so standing anywhere near a Warden seeds fresh Pokémon right on top of it and the timer never counts down. Its heartbeat going calm is not the signal; anger and the burrow timer are separate, and mere detection resets the timer without making it angry.

The fix: back off to 60–120 blocks and wait. Simulation distance is 8 chunks (128 blocks), so the chunk keeps ticking and the Warden keeps running its AI, but you are outside the spawn ring and the Pokémon already near it despawn (64–96 blocks). Measured on this server: at 9 blocks the timer sat pinned at 1200 for four minutes; at 87 blocks it counted straight down and the Warden burrowed 61 seconds later. Clear a room, retreat ~100 blocks for a minute, come back to an empty city.
Tuft of Mew Hair is a trophy. It drops when Mew is summoned and nothing consumes it.
Ultra-rare is not a shiny alert. Spawn Notification only announces Legendary, Mythical, Ultra Beast, Paradox, Shiny and Max-IV. An ultra-rare like Umbreon spawns silently.
CobbleCuisine is an alpha build. It is the only version compatible with Cobblemon 1.7; the stable one crashes the server.
Some Legendary Monuments biomes are spawn-dead - Distortion World, Hall of Origin and Reawakening Sanctum have no Cobblemon spawn tags, so nothing spawns in them.
